# PagePulsar — keyword research (Nov 2026)

Markets: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia. Language: US English site-wide, with UK terms ("website health check") worked into copy.

Method: Google autosuggest (US, UK, CA, AU) for real demand signals, then a look at who ranks for each term. No paid volume tool was used, so this ranks terms by intent and competition, not by exact monthly volume.

## Takeaways

1. Big brands own the informational "checklist" posts for restaurants, but none of them offers a checker. A checker plus a checklist on one page is the gap.
2. Business-type searches lean commercial ("design", "companies", "examples") — the same people who buy a $50 fix. Type pages should mention the fix service clearly.
3. "Mobile friendly test" still has demand with no official Google tool — a dedicated page is a quick win.
4. "Is my website AI ready" is young and growing; low competition.
5. "Website health check" is the UK/AU wording; it goes in the homepage title.
