Honest website checkups for the businesses that keep towns running

PagePulsar gives restaurants, dentists, salons, contractors and repair shops a clear, free answer to one question: is my website helping or hurting?

Why PagePulsar exists

Most website reports are written for marketers. Local business owners get a long PDF full of terms like "canonical tags" and "render-blocking resources", and no idea what to do first.

PagePulsar takes a different view. A local business website has a few simple jobs: show up on Google, look trustworthy, load fast on a phone, and make it easy to call, book or visit. So that's what we check, in plain English, with the most important fixes first.

Who's behind it

PagePulsar is built and run by Shoaib Ahmad, an independent web developer who has spent about three years building and fixing websites for clients around the world through Fiverr. The checker is free. If you'd like help fixing what it finds, you can message Shoaib directly — fixes start from $50.

Our promises

  • The checkup is free, with no signup.
  • We only read your public homepage — never logins or private data.
  • We say what we can't check, instead of pretending.
  • No pushy sales calls. You message us if you want help.
