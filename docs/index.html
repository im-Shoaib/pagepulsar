A website health check built for local businesses

Most free website graders are built for marketers. They score keyword density and backlinks, then hand you a 40-page report. Local businesses need something simpler: can customers find you, trust you, and call or book in a few taps?

PagePulsar checks exactly that. It loads your homepage like a customer would, runs Google's mobile speed test, and looks for the details people need — phone, hours, location, booking, reviews — plus the basics Google and AI search tools rely on. You get one score, a grade, and a short list of fixes ordered by impact.

It works for any small business website, with extra checks for restaurants, dentists, salons and spas, contractors and auto repair shops.

Is PagePulsar really free?

Yes. The checkup is free with no signup. You only pay if you want help fixing what it finds.

Do you need my login or password?

No. PagePulsar only reads your public homepage, the same way a customer or Google does.

How is my score worked out?

About 35 checks in seven areas, each weighted by how much it affects calls and bookings. Customer essentials count most. See how scoring works for the full details.

My site is on Wix, Squarespace or GoDaddy. Does it work?

Yes. Some checks may show "couldn't verify" on site builders that load content with scripts, and you're never marked down for that.

Is this a legal accessibility audit?

No. It's an automated check that finds common problems. It's not legal advice or an accessibility certification.

Who fixes the problems?

You can fix them yourself using the plain-English tips, or send your report on WhatsApp and Shoaib will fix them, starting from $50.
