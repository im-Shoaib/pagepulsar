The contractor website checklist

Homeowners look for these before they call. PagePulsar checks each one.

  1. Services clearly listedRepairs, installs, replacements — name every service so Google and customers can match you.
  2. Towns you serveList your service area cities or counties. It's one of the strongest local search signals you control.
  3. Free estimate buttonA clear "get a free estimate" button or form, at the top of the page.
  4. Licensed and insuredSay it, and show your license number where your state requires it.
  5. Tap-to-call phone numberMost emergency calls come from phones. One tap should start the call.
  6. Emergency service, if you offer it24/7 or same-day service is a big reason people pick you. Make it obvious.
  7. Photos of real jobsBefore-and-after photos of your own work beat stock images every time.
  8. Reviews near the topHomeowners trust other homeowners. Show a few reviews early.
  9. Contractor details for GooglePlumber, Roofing Contractor, Electrician or HVAC structured data helps Google understand your business.
  10. Fast on mobileA slow site loses the caller with a burst pipe to the next result.

Common mistakes we see

Each one quietly sends customers to a competitor.

No service area anywhere

Without town names, Google has a hard time showing you to nearby customers.

Phone number as an image

It can't be tapped, copied or read by search engines.

Contact form only on one page

Put the estimate button where people decide — the homepage.

Outdated photos and © year

Customers wonder if you're still in business.
